# Client Questionnaire

This questionnaire gathers the information needed to configure your Content Systems Platform instance. It is owned by your content owner — the person who will administer the platform and govern content standards across your organization.

Some sections may require input from other team members. The content owner is responsible for identifying who should complete each section, gathering their responses, and returning the completed questionnaire before the follow-up meeting with Keri.

**How to use this document:**
- Review every section before distributing
- Note which sections need input from others and assign them accordingly
- Return the completed questionnaire at least 48 hours before your follow-up meeting
- If a question doesn't apply to your organization, note that explicitly rather than leaving it blank

## Section 3: Content scope
*Complete this section yourself, or with input from team leads who own specific content areas.*

This section defines the content domains your platform instance will cover. A domain is a distinct area of content with its own owner, audience, and governance needs — for example, marketing, product, documentation, or customer communications.

**3.1** List the content types your organization produces. For each, note roughly how much content is produced and how frequently.

Examples: marketing site copy, email campaigns, product UI copy, help documentation, social media, internal communications, sales materials, customer support responses.

**3.2** Which of these content types are in scope for the platform at launch?

**3.3** Are there content types you'd like to include later but not at launch? Note them here so we can plan for them.

**3.4** Based on your content scope, list the domains you think should exist in your platform instance. For each domain, describe it briefly.

A domain groups related content types under a single ownership area. If you're unsure how to group them, list your content types and we'll define domains together in the follow-up meeting.

## Section 5: Users and roles

- **Content owner/admin** — you. Full access, full override rights, all log visibility.
