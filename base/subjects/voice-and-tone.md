# Voice and Tone

## In scope
The distinction between voice and tone, common tone registers and when to use them, applying tone to specific content contexts. Every client instance ships with a Keri-defined default voice and tone. If a client wants something different, it is customized during the discovery engagement and replaces the default entirely for that instance.

## Out of scope
Brand identity (logo, visual language, naming), marketing strategy, campaign copywriting. Clients do not self-configure voice and tone — customization is always done in collaboration with Keri as part of a consulting engagement.
